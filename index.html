Printing and downloads need this page opened from a file or from your own web address. The claude.ai viewer blocks them. ZPL, .prn and ESC/POS can go straight to a printer in Chrome or Edge. ESC/POS conversion keeps text, alignment and bold, and leaves out images and barcodes.

How it works and assumptions

Edit the template or the sample data and the receipt redraws as you type. Reads POS <Commands> XML, raw ESC/POS .prn files, Zebra ZPL labels with loops, DevExpress .repx reports, Epson ePOS-Print tags and a simple <bill> layout. Click any part of the receipt to jump to its line in the XML. Download and Print buttons appear when this page is opened as a local file.

Assumptions for POS Commands XML: Large and Medium use a 12 x 24 dot font (48 characters on 80 mm), small uses 9 x 17 dots (64 characters), SetPosition and SetLeftMargin are in dots, PrintBarcodeImage prepares the image that PrintImageDefined prints, and a missing LineFeed means Yes. Real printers can differ in details. Barcodes in the other layouts are placeholders.
